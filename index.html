📅 Our Production Process

A streamlined workflow designed for speed and quality

Day 1: Discovery Call & Brief

Understand your product, target audience, brand messaging, and specific video goals. Collect all necessary product details, images, and brand guidelines.

Day 2: Concept & Script Creation

Develop 3-5 creative concepts and scripts tailored to your audience. Present options and gather your feedback on preferred direction and style.
